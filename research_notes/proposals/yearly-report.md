# Yearly report (proposal, 2026-11-16)

Idea from Lunch Money / YNAB (the original plan's "extras"), chosen by the owner on 2026-11-16 (gaps plan D8).
**Built 2026-11-16** (`0b7305e`): Trends → Year.

## What it is

One page per year: what came in, what went out, how much was saved, where the money went, and how the year compared
with the one before. Today Trends has monthly series and a year-over-year line, but no single year-in-review.

## What it shows (all from the month summaries, the same numbers as the Now screen and Trends)

1. **The year in one line:** income, spending, saved (income − spending, plus money moved to savings), savings rate.
2. **Month by month:** a bar per month (in / out), the best and the worst month.
3. **Where it went:** groups and their top categories, with the change against the previous year.
4. **Top merchants:** the 10 biggest by total, with the number of charges.
5. **Fixed costs:** the year's subscriptions and fixed expenses with their yearly cost, new ones and ended ones, and
   price changes during the year.
6. **Big moments:** the largest purchases, instalment plans started, events (from the Events screen).
7. **Net worth:** start and end of the year (balances, card debt, holdings), where history exists.

The current year shows "so far" and its months to date. A year needs at least one full month of data.

## Where it shows

A "Year" view in Trends (year picker), and a printable version (browser print to PDF). Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Cost

Medium: mostly a view over existing summaries; the fixed-costs section reads the recurring streams' history.
